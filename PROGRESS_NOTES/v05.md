# Motivation for V05

V04 was designed to defeat shortcut learning on adversarial cues (the failure mode V03 exhibited). The held-out HDA evaluation showed it **did not work**:

- V04 IID validation F1: 99.92% (saturated, uninformative).
- V04 template_ood F1: 86.9% (13-point gap → template memorization).
- V04 on `sample_hdas.py` (the OOD test V04 was built for): **34 errors** vs V03's 38 — a marginal improvement that does not justify deployment.
- Recall: perfect (0 missing entities across all 7 HDAs). The model finds every symptom.
- Precision: failed. 23 of 34 errors are SYMPTOM_NEG hallucinations on non-symptom noun phrases.

Three diagnosable, addressable failure modes (full analysis in `PROGRESS_NOTES/v04.md`):

1. **"Denial cue → next NP is a symptom" shortcut.** Every training negation template fills `{SYMPTOM_NEG}` with a symptom; no distractor template pairs a denial cue with a non-symptom NP. Result: `denies known drug allergies`, `denies previous comorbidities`, `no recent travel history`, `denies chronic diseases` etc. all get tagged SYMPTOM_NEG at inference.
2. **Boundary overreach on unseen modifiers.** Modifiers in HDA text (`sudden onset of`, `intense`, `diffuse`, `increased`, `of moderate intensity`) never appear in any training template, so the model sweeps them into the entity span. Compounded by multi-word `SYMPTOM_POOL` entries that train the model to expect 2–4-token symptom phrases.
3. **Polarity inversion on mid-clause negation.** Cues `X without Y`, `mentioned an absence of X`, `Neither X, Y, nor Z`, `does not present any signs of X` are absent from training. The model overfits to a closed set of cues (`denies`, `no`, `currently without`, `ROS negative`, `Confirmed absence of`).

# What IS changing for V05

### P1 — Adversarial-O distractors: "Denied non-symptom history" (highest impact)

New 9th distractor category in `v05/dataset_templates.py`. ~25 templates pairing denial/negation cues with non-symptom history phrases drawn from a new `NON_SYMPTOM_HISTORY_POOL`. **All tokens labeled O.** This is the direct counter-example training the model has never seen.

Pool: `drug allergies`, `previous comorbidities`, `recent travel`, `past surgeries`, `chronic illnesses`, `regular medications`, `similar episodes`, `triggering event`, `recent changes to medication`, `recent changes to diet`, `continuous medication use`, `prior episodes`.

Cue patterns (each filled with the pool above): `Patient denies {NSH}.`, `No {NSH} reported.`, `Denies known {NSH}.`, `No history of {NSH}.`, `Currently without {NSH}.`, `Patient is not currently using {NSH}.`, `No {NSH} at this time.`, `Denies any {NSH}.`

**Projected impact:** eliminates ~18 of 23 false-positive errors (~70% of all HDA errors).

### P2 — Mid-clause negation patterns in HDA_TEMPLATES

New HDA templates exercising the cue paraphrases V04 never saw. Each template includes 1–2 of the following constructions, mixed with standard POS/NEG content:

- `{SYMPTOM_POS} without {SYMPTOM_NEG}` (e.g. "nausea without vomiting")
- `mentioned an absence of {SYMPTOM_NEG_1} or {SYMPTOM_NEG_2}`
- `Neither {SYMPTOM_NEG_1}, {SYMPTOM_NEG_2}, nor {SYMPTOM_NEG_3}`
- `does not present any signs of {SYMPTOM_NEG_1}, {SYMPTOM_NEG_2}, or {SYMPTOM_NEG_3}`
- `{SYMPTOM_POS} but no {SYMPTOM_NEG}`

**Projected impact:** eliminates the 2 polarity errors and broadens robustness on unseen negation paraphrases.

### P3 — Modifier-vocabulary coverage in AFFIRMED + HDA templates

Add templates where modifiers (`sudden onset of`, `intense`, `diffuse`, `increased`, `progressive`, `worsening`, `of moderate intensity`) appear adjacent to `{SYMPTOM_POS}`. Since the generator labels only the placeholder fill, modifiers will be labeled O — teaching the model not to absorb them.

**Projected impact:** eliminates ~9 boundary overreach errors.

### Combined projection

29 of 34 HDA errors (85%) are directly addressed by P1+P2+P3. The remaining 5 are scattered boundary/tokenization issues that may resolve as a side effect of broader vocabulary coverage.

## Success criteria

- HDA error count < 10 (down from 34).
- Behavioural-set errors stay below V03's 38 baseline.
- Template OOD F1 gap to validation < 5 points (was 13 in V04).
- No regression on recall (V04 had 0 missing entities — must maintain).

## What is NOT changing

- Same model: `dmis-lab/biobert-base-cased-v1.1`, full backbone fine-tuning.
- Same hyperparameters: epoch=3, lr=5e-5, batch=16.
- Same architecture: joint BIO head, 5 labels (O / B/I-SYMPTOM_POS / B/I-SYMPTOM_NEG).
- Same train/eval split structure (template_ood + symptom_ood held out).
- Same evaluation protocol (HDA error count is the headline KPI, not IID F1).

### Residual failure modes (3)

**1. Affirmative-cue FP on non-symptom NPs (3 of 8 errors).** P1 covered denial-side; affirmative-side never added.
- hda6 `"reports nausea and anorexia, with reduced food intake"` → `reduced food` FP
- hda6 `"mentions recent exposure to mosquitoes"` → `mosquitoes` FP
- hda8 `"located diffusely throughout the abdomen"` → `the abdomen` FP

**2. Clause-edge / wide-scope negation (2 errors).** P2 missed trailing/wide-scope frames.
- medium_rash `"allergic reaction; cyanosis is denied"` → polarity inversion on `allergic reaction` (trailing `is denied`)
- medium_rash `"No change in skin texture or inflammation is reported"` → `inflammation` predicted NEG

**3. Edge cases (3 errors, 1 each).**
- hda8 typo `"absense"` predicted POS (P2 cue token unrecognized)
- hda9 literal word `"symptom"` tagged with BIO conflict (caused by dict entries like `"infant symptom"`)
- hda7 `"mild fever"` → predicted just `"fever"` (P3 modifier-O training pulled "mild" out of gold span)

Mechanism (direct inference): on held-out P3 templates the model **glues modifier INTO the entity span** (`"sudden onset of headache"` instead of `"headache"`) → seqeval strict span matching → F1 ≈ 0. P3 generalized inside HDA contexts but did NOT transfer to bare AFFIRMED. V04-era 0.68 has a separate cause (modifier-gluing does NOT explain it — `"Reports ongoing fever since yesterday"` predicts just `"fever"` correctly).

### Audit — code is clean

`skip_nsh_only`, split logic, and WordPiece alignment all verified correct. No truncation. Composition-artifact hypothesis (2 of 5 affirmed holdouts are V05 P3) refuted — V04-era held-out also regressed.

### Carry-forward to V06

1. **Drop the 8 bare P3 AFFIRMED templates** (`v05/dataset_templates.py` lines 90–97). Rely on HDA T25/T26/T28 (which transferred) for modifier-O training. Add explicit boundary-discipline counter-examples (modifier appearing WITHOUT a symptom adjacent, labeled O).
2. **Affirmative-cue → non-symptom NP distractors (P1-symmetric).** Mirror of V05 P1, with NPs from a structurally diverse pool (anatomy, food, exposure, administrative). Closes 3 of 8 errors.
3. **Investigate V04-era affirmed F1 = 0.68 first.** Per-template-idx breakdown on holdouts [1, 7, 17] to localize the regression before committing to (1) and (2).
4. **Out of scope:** symptom dict cleanup (1 error), typo robustness (1 error).
