# Motivation for v04

V03 reached **99.97% test F1 in 2 epochs** with `eval_loss` already rising at epoch 2 — and still fails the easy medical-transcription stress test. That gap is the whole story: the model is not learning clinical language, it is **memorizing 109 templates in two passes** and then has nothing left to learn.

The architecture (joint BIO head with `O` / `SYMPTOM_POS` / `SYMPTOM_NEG`) is not the problem. The V032 ablation already confirmed the backbone must be fine-tuned (I am still not entirely sure about this). The bottleneck is **training-data complexity vs. deployment complexity**:

So the model has never seen the surface patterns that dominate real notes. It is doing **shortcut learning** (Geirhos et al. 2020) on stereotyped template cues — and those cues do not survive distribution shift.

## What IS changing for V04

### Template redesign — [`v04/dataset_templates.py`](../v04/dataset_templates.py)

Four template groups (down from V03's flat list):

1. **`AFFIRMED_TEMPLATES` (40)** — single-symptom, patient-affirmed. Diverse affirmation cues ("acknowledges", "endorses", "complains of", "has been experiencing", "reports", etc.) — not just "patient has X".
2. **`NEGATED_TEMPLATES` (40)** — single-symptom, current denial only. **Past-history negations removed** ("no prior history of X" → out of scope, labeled O via distractor group). Cue diversity: "denies", "ROS negative for", "currently without", "physical exam negative for", "confirmed absence of", "there is no indication of", etc.
3. **`DISTRACTOR_TEMPLATES` (38)** — non-patient context, all `O` labels. 8 categories: generic/educational, epidemiological, hypothetical, family history, past-resolved, discharge instructions, clinical reasoning/differential, uncertain/rule-out. Designed to break the "symptom-word-anywhere → label it" shortcut.
4. **`HDA_TEMPLATES` (20)** — multi-sentence clinical paragraphs mixing POS/NEG in the same example (Tier 2: 3–4 sentences; Tier 3: 4–6 sentences). Indexed placeholders (`{SYMPTOM_POS_1}`, `{SYMPTOM_NEG_2}`...) so the builder draws different symptoms per slot. Deliberately diversified along **6 axes** to prevent surface-pattern shortcuts:
   - **POS/NEG ratio**: 6 POS-heavy + 7 balanced + 7 NEG-heavy (not all "more affirmed than denied")
   - **Ordering**: POS→NEG, NEG→POS, NEG→POS→NEG, interleaved — no single ordering > 35% of templates
   - **Demographics**: pediatric, adolescent, adult, elderly, no-demographics; varied comorbidities
   - **Informant**: patient, mother, father, daughter, son, husband, caregiver, GP referral
   - **Vocab**: each template uses 2–4 distinct affirmation + negation cue phrases
   - **Hold-out separation**: phrasings distinct from `sample_hdas.py` hda6–10 (real OOD eval set)

A new skill `.claude/skills/generate_hda_template/SKILL.md` codifies the rubric so future HDA additions stay balanced.

### Generator redesign — [`data_preparation/dataset_generator.py`](../data_preparation/dataset_generator.py)

- **Two-pass pipeline**: raw generation (JSONL) → tokenization with BIO labels (JSONL). Tokenization records `not_found.jsonl` for any symptom_text that failed to match a token subsequence (debugging signal).
- **Single-symptom groups**: exhaustive — one sample per symptom × per template.
- **HDA group**: `K` random draws per template. `K=int` for a fixed budget, `K=None` to use the pool size.
- **Cross-polarity dedup**: same symptom cannot fill both POS and NEG slots in the same HDA (was a bug in earlier sketches).

### Hold-out evaluation set — [`sample_hdas.py`](../sample_hdas.py)

5 real-style HDAs (`hda6`–`hda10`) annotated with character spans, format-matched to the existing `transcriptions` list. Stylistic gaps documented as a TODO (NEG-first openings, demographic openings, varied cue vocab, family-as-informant variants).

### Evaluation strategy (for the V04 training run)

To diagnose template / cue-word overfitting — not just track IID F1:

1. **Template hold-out eval**: 5 affirmed + 5 negated + 4 distractor + 2 HDA templates held out (`template_ood` split, 12,262 samples). Train on the rest, eval F1 separately. Gap = template overfitting.
2. **Symptom hold-out eval**: 20 symptoms held out (`symptom_ood` split, 2,440 samples). Train on the remaining 873, eval on held-out. Gap = symptom overfitting.
3. **OOD eval**: `sample_hdas.py` hda6–10 + the existing easy/medium transcription cases. Primary qualitative test.
4. **Behavioral probes**: 15–20 hand-written sentences with cue phrasings absent from training ("she has not experienced X", "without any X", "X — none") — catches cue-word brittleness that F1 will miss.


### WordPiece alignment — [`data_preparation/wordpiece_alignment.py`](../data_preparation/wordpiece_alignment.py)

New module. Reads the three word-level tokenized JSONL files and produces
subword-aligned versions ready for training:

- `align_sample()`: tokenizes with `is_split_into_words=True`, maps word-level
  BIO labels to subwords via `word_ids()`. Special tokens → `-100`. First subword
  inherits word label. Continuation subwords convert `B-` → `I-`.
- `align_file()`: runs `align_sample` over a full JSONL, reports truncation count.
- Label set hardcoded (`O`, `B/I-SYMPTOM_POS`, `B/I-SYMPTOM_NEG`) — not discovered
  from data, so all three splits share the same `LABEL2ID` mapping.
- Verification script at `v04/data/verify_wordpiece.py` — all checks passed:
  sequence length consistency, BIO continuity, label id range, label distribution,
  K=40 HDA coverage (866/893 = 97% of train symptoms appear in HDA samples).

### HuggingFace upload — [`v04/data/upload_to_hf.py`](../v04/data/upload_to_hf.py)

completed 2026-05-19.

## Success criteria

- **IID test F1 is no longer the headline metric** — it was saturated and uninformative in V03. Behavioural-set error counts (post-fix scorer) are the primary KPI.
- Target: reduce total behavioural errors below V03's post-fix baseline (38), and recover non-trivial entities on the easy transcription.
- Stretch: pass the medium transcription (multi-symptom, mixed polarity in one sentence).

### Diagnosis (three concrete failure modes)

**1. "Denial cue → next NP is a symptom" shortcut (18 of 23 FPs).**
Every one of 40 `NEGATED_TEMPLATES` pairs a denial cue with a symptom; zero of 38 `DISTRACTOR_TEMPLATES` pair a denial cue with a non-symptom NP. The model learned a brittle distributional rule. Examples of hallucinated SYMPTOM_NEG spans on non-symptom NPs:
- `"drug allergies"` (×3 — hda6, hda8, hda10) after `"denies known"`
- `"previous comorbidities"` (×2 — hda6, hda9) after `"denies"`
- `"continuous medication use"` (×2 — hda6, hda9) after `"denies"`
- `"recent travel history"` (hda10), `"recent travel"` (easy), `"chronic diseases"` (hda10), `"chronic illnesses"` (hda8), `"past surgeries"` (hda8), `"regular medications"` (hda7), `"similar episodes"` (easy), `"triggering event"` (easy), `"diet"` (easy), `"recent changes to medication"` (easy), `"inflammation"` (medium_rash)

**2. Boundary overreach on modifiers (9 errors) — vocabulary gap, not labeling inconsistency.**
Generator labels only the placeholder fill; modifiers in templates ("Severe", "Mild", "Moderate") ARE labeled O. But the modifiers appearing in the HDAs never appear in training:
- `"sudden onset of high fever"` (gold: `"high fever"`) — `"sudden onset of"` not in any template
- `"intense headache"` (gold: `"headache"`) — `"intense"` not in any template
- `"diffuse joint pain"` / `"diffuse backache"` (gold: `"joint pain"` / `"backache"`) — `"diffuse"` not in any template
- `"increased fatigue"` (gold: `"fatigue"`) — `"increased"` not in any template
- `"headache of moderate intensity"` (gold: `"headache"`) — postmodifier not in any template

Compounded by multi-word `SYMPTOM_POOL` entries (`"high fever"`, `"prolonged fever"`, `"sudden onset of fever"`) which encourage leftward span extension.

**3. Polarity inversion on mid-clause negation (2 errors) — cue-vocabulary gap.**
- `"nausea without vomiting"` → predicted as one SYMPTOM_POS span. `"X without Y"` mid-clause pattern is absent from training; only `"Currently without {SYMPTOM_NEG}"` (sentence-initial frame) exists.
- `"an absense of dysuria"` → predicted SYMPTOM_POS. `"absence of"` exists in training only in clinical frames (`"Confirmed absence of"`, `"Assessment shows absence of"`), never mid-clause with indefinite article.
- `"Neither X, Y, nor Z"` and `"does not present any signs of"` (hda9) — both absent from training.

### Verdict — Do not deploy. Retrain V05.

Recall is perfect (0 missing entities across all HDAs); the model finds every symptom. **Precision is the problem.** V04 shifted V03's error mode rather than fixing it: V03 missed entities and had brittle cue dependence; V04 finds entities reliably but over-extends and hallucinates on adversarial cues that V04 was specifically designed to defeat.

The IID validation F1 of 99.9% combined with template_ood F1 of 86.9% was the warning sign. The 13-point gap was template memorization; the HDA evaluation confirmed it.

**Carry-forward to V05:** see `PROGRESS_NOTES/v05.md`.
