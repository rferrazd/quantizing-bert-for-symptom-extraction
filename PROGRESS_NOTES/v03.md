# V03: BioBERT symptom NER (expanded synthetic data)

Goal for this version was **not** to chase leaderboard token-F1 alone, but to address failure modes seen on a behavioural set in v02 by **fixing the training distribution**: many more sentence templates, explicit **negative / distractor** contexts (symptom *words* that must **not** be tagged as entities), and harder boundaries. Implementation lives in [`v03/dataset_templates.py`](../v03/dataset_templates.py) 
  > 41 `NEGATED_TEMPLATES`
  > 40 `AFFIRMED_TEMPLATES` 
  > 38 `DISTRACTOR_TEMPLATES`
  > total of 109 templates


Baseline plan from [v02 notes](./v02.md): keep **full fine-tuning** and invest in corpus diversity rather than swapping training style for the boundary-overreach case.

---

## Dataset

- **Hub:** `Rogarcia18/symptoms_ner_v03_biobert`
- **Templates:** expanded affirmed and negated phrasings; **distractor** lines use `{SYMPTOM}` in medical or administrative context but are labeled **all `O`** (no `SYMPTOM_POS` / `SYMPTOM_NEG` span), so the model learns *when not* to extract.
- If you add more “unpolarized” rows later, keep the contract explicit in code/comments: either **all-`O` distractors** or real **POS/NEG** spans—mixing unlabeled gold inside otherwise supervised rows will silently hurt training.
- **Size of the dataset:** 106267 rows (80% training, 10% val, 10% test)

# V03 and V032 Comparison

## Same test split vs behavioural stress test

**Held-out splits (IID with the synthetic pipeline).** With **two epochs of full fine-tuning**, validation and test token-level F1 are ~**0.9997**, i.e. the encoder + head jointly fit the templated POS/NEG distribution almost perfectly. With **frozen BioBERT weights and only the token-classification head** trained (**V032**, 40 epochs), the **same splits** collapse to roughly **59% micro-F1** on both val and test, with **precision ~0.49** and **recall ~0.74**. That imbalance is characteristic of a representation that stays close to LM geometry: the head can tilt toward emitting entity labels often enough to salvage recall, but it cannot cleanly separate nuanced POS vs NEG cues and BIO structure at token resolution without updating the backbone.

These numbers are mutually consistent with the [v02 plan](./v02.md) to rely on **full fine-tuning** for representation learning while improving data.

## Behavioural aggregates (counts are not orthogonal)

Notebook evaluation uses [`behavioural_template_bank.json`](../behavioural_template_bank.json) (categories such as temporal progression, multi-symptom boundaries, attribution, long clinical sentences, and explicit “should NOT extract” clauses). Training surface form is driven by [`v03/dataset_templates.py`](../v03/dataset_templates.py) (109 slot templates: affirmed, negated, all-`O` distractors). There is **partial overlap in intent** (e.g. family history / educational / screening language appears in both training distractors and the behavioural “guardrails” bucket), but the **behavioural bank adds length, conjunction, and multi-entity layout** that the training templates mostly do not mirror. So some gap between near-perfect synthetic metrics and non-zero behavioural errors is **expected domain shift**, not necessarily leakage.

Important interpretive caveat: in `error_analysis/error_categorization.py`, every **false-positive span** (predicted symptom span with no matched gold entity) increments **both** `Irrelevant Span Mislabeling` (type\_0) **and** `Model Overgeneralization` (type\_7). So **`Irrelevant Span Mislabeling` and `Model Overgeneralization` should be read together** as “roughly doubled counting of hallucinated spans,” not as independent failure modes.

With that caveat:

| Reading | **V03 (full model, 2 epochs)** | **V032 (head-only, 40 epochs)** |
|--------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Behavioural totals (sum of listed buckets) | 46 | 63 |
| “Extra” hallucinated-span signal (Irrelevant + Overgeneralization) | 9 + 7 = **16** (≈ duplicate count of FP events, see caveat) | 20 + 19 = **39** |
| Missing expected spans | **8** | **18** |

V032 degrades mainly through **many more hallucinated symptom spans on gold-`O` regions** (aligned with low precision on IID test) **and many more misses** on spans the behavioural suite expects annotated. Boundary overreach drops from **18 → 2** not because span boundaries magically improve: **boundary classification only triggers when overlap with gold is sufficiently high** (~50%+ of expected span in the current heuristic). Severely misplaced or hallucinated spans are often routed to **missing / irrelevant / false-positive** buckets instead.

Polarity mistakes stay small for both (**4 vs 2**); the bottleneck is detection quality, not NEG-vs-POS once a span aligns.

Tokenization/BIO artefacts appear once under V032, consistent with brittle decisions when logits are muddy.

## Conclusions

1. **For this task and corpus, freezing BioBERT while training only the classifier head does not approximate full fine-tuning.** Forty epochs barely move token-level correctness on held-out IID data (~59% vs ~99.9%). The backbone must adapt to templated cues and distractor-vs-mention boundaries.

2. **High synthetic scores alongside non-zero behavioural errors are normal.** The behavioural bank is stylized differently from the synthetic training surface, and the scorer’s false-positive rule double-counts certain errors (see above), so behavioural buckets do not behave like IID token metrics.

3. **Do not conclude from raw counts alone that boundary overreach regressed.** Compare boundary errors only when spans overlap sufficiently to enter the boundary check; otherwise reliance on aggregates misleads — especially comparing a strong matcher (V03) to a fragmented one (V032).

4. **Next behavioural improvements** should focus on narrowing the stylistic gap: add training templates closer to behavioural categories (multi-symptom commas, longer narrative clauses, attribution hedges), or expand gold rules for ambiguous phrases where your scoring expects no span.
